Export PDF Editor settings (old Edge version)

Your toolbar, highlight, comment and AI settings (including the Gemini key) live in Edge's storage for this address, so the new desktop app cannot see them by itself. This page saves them to a small file you can import once.

Then drag pdfeditor-settings.json onto the new PDF Editor shortcut (or its .exe). It asks before importing. Your PDFs are not affected: edits live inside the PDFs themselves.
